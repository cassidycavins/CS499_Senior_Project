**Project Overview:**
The AI-assisted study and academic planning tool is a web-based application designed to help college students organize course materials, study more effectively, and track their academic progress. Students can upload notes and other study materials and use AI-assisted features to generate personalized study resources such as practice questions, quizzes, and flashcards. The system also tracks quiz performance and study progress to help students identify topics that may require additional review.

The goal of the project is to combine study-material organization, AI-assisted study tools, and progress tracking into one centralized platform.
